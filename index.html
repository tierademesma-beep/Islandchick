Kalena Samoa

O le tausaga 2027 — The Year 2027

Twelve months through the flowers, birds, waterfalls and beaches of Samoa — paired with Samoan proverbs and everyday phrases (gagana Samoa) alongside their English meaning.

☀️ Aso Sā (Sunday) is traditionally a sacred day of rest in Samoa — it is marked in coral on every month below.

Upu Fa’aaogā · Words Used in This Calendar

Talofa — hello / greetings
Fa’afetai — thank you
Tōfā soifua — farewell
Aso — day
Māsína — month / moon
Tausaga — year
Fa’a Samoa — the Samoan way of life
‘ōina (okina, ‘) — a glottal-stop letter unique to Samoan spelling
